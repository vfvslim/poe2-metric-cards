## 业务问题 BUSINESS QUESTION

What are PoE2 in-game (MTX) paid users and pay amount by country and platform on a given day?

## 指标定义

In-game payment metrics: who paid (`paid_users`) and how much (`pay_amount`) from in-game MTX spend.

Shown mainly via `app_poe2_pay_flat_d`. **Not** Steam / Xbox / other store revenue.

## 计算公式

Pipeline: `dwd_player_pay_d` → `dwm_full_pay_bitmap_d` → `app_pay_flat_d`

- **DWD:** player-day pay detail
- **DWM:** ever-paid players + `cbitmap` / `cbitmap_reverse`
- **APP:** aggregate paid users / pay amount by date × country × os

Payer flags (paid / new-paid / retention / return / churn style) come from the bitmap.

League overview often uses `sum(pay_amount) / 100` as `ingame_revenue`.

## 口径要素

### 分子 Numerator

- `paid_users`: distinct payers with today’s pay bit set
- `pay_amount`: sum of the day’s pay amount from bitmap / pay flat

### 分母 Denominator

None.

### 粒度 Grain

- APP flat: `dtstatdate` x `country_code` x `os`
- DWD / DWM: player-level (`openid`)

## 过滤条件 FILTERS

- `dtstatdate = '{{ ds }}'`
- Aggregate from the ever-paid bitmap population
- Some dims use `255` / `'255'` as all
- `game_code` = `poe2`

## 维度

- `dtstatdate`
- `country_code`
- `os`

## 踩坑与注意 CAVEATS

- In-game MTX only; **not** Steam / Epic / Xbox / PS / web / Kakao store revenue. See [Platform net revenue](/poe2-metric-cards/core/platform-net-revenue/).
- Payer retention ≠ gameplay DAU retention.
- League `ingame_revenue` often uses `sum(pay_amount) / 100` and is **league-window** scoped. See [League overview](/poe2-metric-cards/league/overview/).
- DWM is ever-paid history, not “paid today only”.
- PoE1 has the same d/m pattern under `poe1_ingame_sales`.

## SQL 模式

Load player pay into DWD → maintain full pay bitmap in DWM → aggregate to `app_pay_flat_d` by date / country / os.
