## 业务问题 BUSINESS QUESTION

What is PoE2 platform / store net revenue by store, country, and product on a given day?

## 指标定义

Platform (store) **net revenue** / net revenue after chargebacks.

## 计算公式

Each store SQL runs first (Steam / Xbox / PlayStation / Epic / official web / Kakao), then union into `app_poe2_platform_revenue_d`.

## 口径要素

### 分子 Numerator

`net_revenue` (and related gross / chargeback / unit fields) aggregated from per-store revenue tables into `app_poe2_platform_revenue_d`.

### 分母 Denominator

None.

### 粒度 Grain

`dtstatdate` x `os` x `country_code` x `product_unified_id` / category.

## 过滤条件 FILTERS

- `dtstatdate = '{{ ds }}'`
- Store identified by `os`:
  - 8 = PlayStation
  - 9 = Xbox
  - 11 = Steam
  - 12 = Epic
  - 13 = Official web
  - 15 = Kakao
- Product eligibility depends on `poe_product_list` / store product-info tables

## 维度

- `dtstatdate`
- `os`
- `country_code`
- `product_unified_id`
- `category`

## 踩坑与注意 CAVEATS

- Platform revenue only; **not** in-game MTX (`app_poe2_pay_flat_d`). See [In-game pay](/poe2-metric-cards/core/ingame-pay-amount/).
- Not the same as league `ingame_revenue` (`sum(pay_amount) / 100` over the league window). See [League overview](/poe2-metric-cards/league/overview/).
- Always say **platform / store** vs **in-game**.
- PoE1 in the focus folder is more Steam-centric; do not assume the same full multi-store union.

## SQL 模式

Build per-store daily revenue tables → union in `app_platform_revenue_d.sql` → land in `app_poe2_platform_revenue_d`.
